# Code of Conduct

FsusBlog is a public-preview engineering project. Participation should stay technical, respectful, and safe for maintainers, users, and external contributors.

## Expected Behavior

- Keep discussion focused on the project, evidence, and concrete tradeoffs.
- Be respectful when disagreeing about implementation, design, security, or performance.
- Use inclusive language and avoid personal attacks, harassment, threats, or sexualized content.
- Do not post private data, real credentials, exploit details, personal email addresses, real IP addresses, production logs, or other sensitive material in public issues, pull requests, comments, screenshots, or examples.
- Report security vulnerabilities through [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md), not public issues.

## Unacceptable Behavior

- Harassment, intimidation, stalking, doxxing, or repeated unwanted contact.
- Discriminatory language or behavior.
- Publishing private vulnerability details before maintainers confirm a disclosure path.
- Posting secrets, tokens, cookies, private infrastructure details, real user data, or unredacted logs.
- Spam, low-effort disruption, or intentionally misleading evidence.

## Enforcement

Maintainers may edit, hide, delete, lock, or move public content that violates this policy, especially when it contains security details or private data. Repeated or severe violations may result in blocked participation.

For security-sensitive reports, follow the private reporting flow in [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).
